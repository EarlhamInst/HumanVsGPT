# Source papers

The 21 published articles whose metadata was independently extracted by a human
curator and by a language model, producing the manifest pairs compared in this study.

Bibliographic details come from the project's own PubMed curation export
(`2025-04-24 plant single-cell literature for curation.xlsx`), so they match the
records the corpus was selected from rather than being re-derived from the PDFs.
That matters: DOIs read out of the PDFs were wrong in at least one case, where
Liu et al. yielded the DOI of a research-highlight article written *about* the
paper rather than the paper itself.

ᵖ marks the two whose PDF is a preprint. Dorrity et al. was read as the bioRxiv
version but is cited here as the published article; Yan et al. was a preprint at
the time of curation. A reader checking manifest values against a published
version may find differences attributable to neither annotator.

The articles themselves are third-party copyright and are not redistributed here.
