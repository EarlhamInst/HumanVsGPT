# Source papers

The 21 published articles whose metadata was independently extracted by a human
curator and by a language model, producing the manifest pairs compared in this
study.

Both annotators worked from the main text of each article as distributed here; no
supplementary material was used, with one documented exception (`FINDINGS.md`, 4.1).
The articles themselves are third-party copyright and are not redistributed with
this repository.

Titles are taken from each PDF's embedded metadata where present and from the
filename otherwise, then normalised for Unicode dashes. Two entries marked
*preprint* are the bioRxiv versions, which is what both annotators read.
